## Notes

- **SmallSort harnesses:** 280 + 75 = 355 on each OS, equal to the ordinary-harness registry of EXT X0 (`661a5a1a`).
  Totals: macOS 2101 = 2080 of L1-8 + 21 L2 `correct.rs` harnesses; Linux 2103 (the std list differs by
  target by two harnesses, as in L1-8).
- **ubuntu 2/4, first attempt:** the runner was shut down after 37 min of the job ("The runner has received a
  shutdown signal", exit 143). By then 266 harnesses had reported SUCCESS and none FAILED; the summary step did not
  run because the timed step was killed, so no memory data exist for that attempt. The job was re-run once, alone,
  after the run finished (owner's approval); the row above is the re-run (attempt 2, 12:56–15:49 UTC). Its largest single process was `kani-driver` (9.8 GiB sampled, 10.1 GiB by `time -v`) with 2.5 GB peak swap and no OOM: with `KANI_JOBS=2` partition 2 is the memory-heaviest Linux job, at about two thirds of the 15.6 GiB runner.
- **macOS is the binding OS, partition 2 the binding job:** 189 min of 360 (1.9×), with 1.2 GB peak swap and no
  OOM. All four macOS jobs got the standard 3 CPU / 7 GiB image this time (in L1-8 partition 3 got a larger one).
- **Runner variance on the same image is large.** Against L1-8 (run 40603213401, candidate `5c713131`, also 3 CPU /
  7 GiB for p2 and p4): macOS p2 went from 104 to 184 min although only 19 L2 harnesses were added to it; its
  slowest harness `network::w2p2_k10_perm_u64_n12` took 750 s then and 1266 s now on unchanged code. macOS p4 went
  the other way, 117 → 76 min, with `merge::w2p2_bm_u64_len16` 803 → 511 s. A single run therefore does not bound
  the job time; the margin above is the worst observed, not a guarantee.
- **Linux (`KANI_JOBS=2`) is not binding:** the longest job is partition 2, 172 min of 360 (2.1×); peak swap 0 except partition 2 (2.5 GB). GCB e2-standard-4 was
  much slower than these runners (its partition 2 timed out at 300 min in stage C) and is calibration only.
- **`KANI_JOBS` in the summaries:** the per-job summary prints "KANI_JOBS unset" for every job because the request field
  `kani_jobs` is empty; the workflow passes `KANI_JOBS=2` to run-kani.sh on Linux through the environment
  (`smallsort-kani-k1.yml`, lines 97–98), as kani.yml does.
- **Memory:** the sampled sum of RSS (macOS 11.6–12.2 GiB, Linux 5.0–8.8 GiB) over-counts shared pages and is not a
  memory figure; single-process RSS and swap are the signals.
- **Scope:** these are the eight ordinary partitions of `kani.yml` only. Autoharness, list/metrics, `rustc.yml` and
  the other PR workflows were not run; that is item П-6 of the R8 submission addendum (`a03f8c3a`).
