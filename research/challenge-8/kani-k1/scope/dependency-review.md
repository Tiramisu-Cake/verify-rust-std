## Allocation and claim limits

- `insert_tail` contract requires a valid, initialized, writable, same-allocation `[begin,tail]` with sorted prefix; ensures sorted region and unchanged probe count. The u8 prefix and u64 start harnesses satisfy that same-allocation shape. `correct::scratch_insert_contract` exercises one initialized 48-element allocation at starts `{0,6,7}`, lengths `5..=8`; both types are opt-in in B. Do not claim ordinary scratch coverage from either instance or claim scratch geometry from the prefix/17-element proofs alone.
- `bidirectional_merge` requires a source slice of length `2..=32`, sorted halves, and a valid disjoint writable destination; it ensures sorted output and probe-count preservation. Exact-N contract harnesses do not by themselves establish that the real general/stable caller’s 48-element scratch region and call-site offsets meet the contract. Preserve the composed route witness or explicitly narrow the claim to the contract harness’s exact source/destination shape.
- `sort4_stable` is proved on exact four-element source plus separate four-element destination. `sort4_arbitrary_comparator_*` are real-body safety/value-preservation checks; they do not prove ordered sort correctness for arbitrary comparators.
- `swap_if_less` requires distinct positions and valid read/write access in one allocation. `proof_for_contract` proves its stated local contract; it does not establish a caller’s index selection unless a caller harness remains.
- A general `stub_verified` caller proves its assertions only under the verified callee contract. Dropping the matching `(function, type, comparator, length/region)` contract witness means relabel the caller as conditional/compositional evidence, or move it together. It does not turn into a real-body proof.

## Historical selection rationale and B decision

Times are source rows in `c3c-inputs/mac-times.tsv` at the P-6 basis (`6cfc76cb`), not fresh measurements. Useful first cuts, retaining safety/trait representatives:

1. **Do not label insertion permutation as subsumed by network correctness.** `network_correctness_fixed` invokes the real unstable trait/body, proves sortedness at all N, and checks full-value multiplicity only for N<9. For N≥9 its comment describes composition with insertion-tail permutation, but the two harness assertions are not composed on one output; `insertion_permutation` checks the suffix only. The costly `insertion_permutation_u8_n12` (217.2 s) and `insertion_permutation_u64_n15` (215.7 s) can be moved only with the explicit loss of those type/length suffix-permutation witnesses. Neither has a `stub_verified` dependency.
2. **Exact-array swap contract width duplicate:** keep a large-array case selected for type/safety value, but move one of `swap_contract_bytes85_n2` (200.8 s) / `swap_contract_bytes86_n2` (398.6 s) to controls. If dispatch threshold evidence is desired, retain `smoke::predicate_efficient_swap_type_matrix` plus the less expensive width, and state that the omitted exact width has no contract instance.
3. **Upper general-route property is composition evidence, not a direct-body duplicate.** `general_stable_sorted_permutation_u64_n12` (337.5 s), `_u64_n16` (251.6 s), and `_u8_n13` (204.1 s) are candidates to move as whole `(T,N)` groups with matching merge contract if ordinary budget demands it. B deliberately retains lower general/stable merge-stub callers with matching T/N/comparator proofs, while labeling exact-N↔scratch48 transfer conditional. All `correct.rs` stub callers (`fallback_stub` u8/u64 and baseline ordinary u8 general/stable N14/15) and the u8 scratch contract are opt-in; direct u8 prefix remains ordinary. State loss of each exact arbitrary-input sortedness + probe-multiplicity route claim; direct contracts do not replace it.
4. Keep representative **real trait and safety** checks (`types::*` trait-entry checks, mutating/non-Freeze/Cell/drop paths, and dispatch/network safety at boundary lengths) ahead of more same-route full-width correctness points. The 17/32 boundaries and max-size safety cases are distinct claims, not cheap duplicates merely because adjacent sizes exist.

**Minimum useful independent merge-contract set:** retain `merge_contract_u8_n2_to4` and `merge_contract_u64_n2_to4` ordinary as direct, no-caller checks of both element types across the base lengths. This supports only those instances; do not describe it as a general length-2..16 proof. If ordinary needs an upper-size witness, add both exact N16 harnesses and describe the sparse `{2,3,4,16}` domain, or keep the full type/length families in opt-in. B selects u64 N2..8 and u8 N2..9 exact merge domains including the grouped base proofs. `merge.rs` real-body fallback witnesses are independent; only N0..6 remain ordinary B.

Source cost context: 322 macOS rows matching `verify::*`, sum 25,367.5 s; families: `network` 13,269.2 s, `merge` 9,708.8 s, `correct` 2,318.0 s. The three allocation-sensitive insert-tail rows among the most expensive are u64 starts 6 (584.9 s), 2 (368.4 s), 7 (326.7 s); do not remove them individually while keeping claims that rely on those region witnesses. Upper network safety N31/32 are one full boundary group in the C3c split plan; their split timing is unconfirmed without fresh measurements, so prefer the whole group opt-in pending measurement. No single source audit establishes proof portability across Kani allocation models; that is the material uncertainty and should be resolved by a covers/contract audit in the patch review.
