Title: Keep delayed MIR instrumentation local to each model

Refs #3300

Depends on `PR-06` and, transitively, #5172. Until the dependencies merge, the diff against `main` includes the earlier stack commits.

Delayed uninitialized-memory analysis rewrites cached MIR bodies. When another harness reuses one of those bodies, it can inherit checks inserted for the previous harness. This makes the generated checks depend on which harness was compiled first.

### Change

- Journal bodies changed by the delayed pass and restore their previous cached state when the model's codegen scope ends, including error exits.
- Keep ordinary and stub transformations intact; restore only delayed-pass mutations.
- Apply the scope at both CPROVER and LLBC codegen boundaries.

Six cache tests and two scope tests pass. Eleven completed compiler invocations give 18 exact mixed-versus-isolated comparisons; expected read failures and cycle diagnostics remain present. All four oracle-sensitivity mutations are detected.

The final tree `9829702c1aa0ecb73c860fc0ad5451757917d6cb` is identical to the tested export at `ad499e41dd`: the full `scripts/kani-regression.sh` [passed on ubuntu-24.04, ubuntu-24.04-arm and macos-15](https://github.com/Tiramisu-Cake/kani/actions/runs/43665002636). On that same tree, [bounded SmallSort replay](https://github.com/Tiramisu-Cake/kani/actions/runs/43683557088) with closure comparators passed two merge cases and retained the expected `select_unpredictable` boundary in sort4. These runs qualify the final tree, not the earlier stack heads.

### Limitations

This isolates instrumentation; it does not expand the initialization model. Indirect calls, nested by-value union/`MaybeUninit` aggregates, shadow transfer under `stub_verified` or `kani::recursion`, analysis call cycles and unmodeled intrinsics remain limitations. The replay does not establish support for the whole SmallSort module.
