The remaining supported edges need regression coverage, and the documentation must make the model's conservative boundaries clear.

### Change

- Add 11 edge fixtures covering union constants, raw projected-field neighbors and padding, by-reference DST cases, and by-value unsized transfers explicitly rejected as unsupported.
- Complete the contract and Rust feature-support documentation updates.
- Make no production-code changes; this change only adds tests and documentation.

### Testing

All four sensitivity mutations were detected by the runner. This is not the full three-platform gate; that qualification applies to the final tree after the remaining dependency is applied.

### Limitations

Supported cases remain `MaybeUninit<T>` slots, flat arrays/slices of slots, `MaybeUninit<[T; N]>`, computable masks and supported direct MIR calls. Shadow transfer under `stub_verified` or `kani::recursion`, indirect calls, nested by-value union/`MaybeUninit` aggregates, analysis cycles and unmodeled intrinsics remain unsupported or documented conservative cases. `select_unpredictable` still blocks verification of the whole SmallSort module.
