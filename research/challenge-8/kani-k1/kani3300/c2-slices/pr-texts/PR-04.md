Title: Track MaybeUninit storage across copies and direct calls

Refs #3300

Depends on `PR-03` and, transitively, #5172. Until the dependencies merge, the diff against `main` includes the earlier stack commits.

Scratch storage can contain initialized payload and uninitialized padding in `MaybeUninit`. The experimental `-Z uninit-checks` model needs to preserve that distinction through copies, moves and supported direct calls. Marker-dispatched backend hooks bypass the MIR return store and previously reached an internal assertion; they now receive an explicit unsupported-transfer diagnostic.

### Change

- Pair live-source caller snapshots with callee-entry loads and a separate normal-return transfer, preserving known-uninitialized bytes across Copy and Move.
- Preserve raw byte state through raw copies; typed transfers preserve payload bytes at the actual slot stride and discard padding state.
- Conservatively reset nested by-value aggregate destinations and Move sources; Copy sources remain intact. This may cause false positives and does not add recursive field transfer.
- Add storage, padding, pairing, nested-reset and actual hook/model admission controls.

### Testing

Cases cover paired legal/hole transfers, Copy/Move, padding, nested resets and hook admission, and exercise deferred cycle, ordinary-entry and raw-equality/layout cases from `PR-01`/`PR-03`. Captured contract and generated-modifies integration passed later in [run 43715788377](https://github.com/Tiramisu-Cake/kani/actions/runs/43715788377), not on this head.

### Limitations

Support covers a `MaybeUninit<T>` slot, `[MaybeUninit<T>; N]`, `[MaybeUninit<T>]`, and `MaybeUninit<[T; N]>` with computable masks. Shadow transfer under `stub_verified` or `kani::recursion`, indirect calls, nested by-value union/`MaybeUninit` aggregates, analysis cycles and unmodeled intrinsics remain limitations. In particular, `select_unpredictable` still blocks verification of the whole SmallSort module.
