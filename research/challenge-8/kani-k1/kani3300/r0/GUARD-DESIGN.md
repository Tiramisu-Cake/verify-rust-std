# KANI3300 R0 — alternatives for owner review (proposed only)

The inventory in NOTES.md uses exact B/R1/R2 commit paths and line aliases. No guard is implemented; no option is selected. R1 already admits flat storage; R2 refines masks/protocol and adds full-extent raw_eq checks. Reverting raw_eq alone cannot establish safe release of that expanded domain.

1. Proposed recognition boundary: distinguish outer lang-item `MaybeUninit<T>` (W, including `MaybeUninit<[T;N]>`) from outer Array/Slice of MU slots (F/S), before projection, aggregate/repeat expansion, conversion or call scheduling. Keep unsupported compound layouts separate; do not classify every array inside W as flat. Existing `maybe_uninit_storage` conflates these results (L:110–128), so removing its flat cases would create silent fallback paths.
2. Proposed origin boundary: inspect storage roots, projection prefixes, local declarations/signatures, referenced static/constant storage and source/destination types at the earliest reachable MIR use, before flat indexing, unsize, raw address, pointer cast/offset or cross-call escape erases F/S shape. An element `*mut MU<T>` can originate in either W or F; its leaf type is insufficient. Reject a known flat root before its first effect/escape; retain classification for later uses or conservatively reject unresolved origins. This is an unsupported-domain boundary, not a type-only raw_eq provenance rule or a new provenance model.
3. Proposed common backstop: validate original shape/origin of both copy operands and effective check/set range at entry to I::build_check_for_operation, before BeginArgumentTransfer (I:167), constant materialization (189), operand_ty invariants (227), SetRef(false) for_deinit (244), check exemptions (251–258) or SetRef(true) return (262). Carry pre-erasure shape context to this boundary; a byte-array CheckAs target or expanded per-slot AssignUnion alone cannot reconstruct it. Guard Copy/SetSliceChunk, Set/SetStorage/CreateUnion/AssignUnion, every check family and Store/Load; no syntactic Copy-only patch.
4. Proposed visitor backstop: at V::visit_statement/visit_rvalue and find_all/visit_terminator, reject F/S before assignment/aggregate/repeat/conversion expansion, entry loads, Begin/Store/snapshot/reset and return0 scheduling. Cover projected raw-pointer destinations, union/storage effects, constants, zero-element loops, direct-slot aliases derived from flat roots, return-only and arg-only calls. Pointer/reference arguments require pointee-effect/origin coverage; their ordinary pointer value transfer is not a shadow payload transfer.
5. Proposed diagnostic chain: queued MemoryInitOp::Unsupported → I:219–221,824–831 → F::insert_check(UnsupportedCheck, None) (F:195–233,515–517) → UnsupportedCheckHook → emitted assert/assume-false PropertyClass::UnsupportedConstruct (H:202–235). This safety boundary must be reachable before the unsupported effect/check/transfer; a warning, skipped layout or dropped target is insufficient. Exact emitted/reached property remains NOT_RUN.
6. Proposed retention requirement: collect operands once and flush each instruction exactly once on every exit, preserving earlier reads and entry loads. Anchor empty entry/terminator-only bodies at the actual terminator (V:47–95); flush last statements before replacing placeholders. Fix/equivalently close unresolved-call early return V:493–502 before relying on any guard. Insert Unsupported Before the effect rather than inheriting Copy/Set After positioning; F:287–306 supports a terminator anchor, while After a terminal instruction may have no usable edge. Check Return/Abort/Unreachable placement explicitly.
7. Proposed analysis ordering requirement: recognition must run independently of delayed seeds and be safe before analyses that can fail on unresolved calls; delayed U:91–92 is conditional, S:134–139 resolves with unwrap, P points_to_analysis.rs:448–459 unwraps callee body. Preserve a reviewable Unsupported through fallible analysis handling; if it cannot be guaranteed, block release for that path. A runtime guard does not prevent a prior analysis ICE. B1 repair remains separate implementation work, not performed by R0.

**A — direct MU export, if owner chooses it.** Preserve supported W typed D(T) masks, constant-mask intersection, false source data, padding clearing, raw-vs-typed distinction and arg/return pairing; retain the general mk_cast/CheckAs path used by raw-pointer→reference checks. Restore legacy raw_eq policy only with complete F/S rejection and disclosure of its residual preexisting byte-read gap. No acceptance is inferred from legacy equality success.
Proposed A rejection covers whole F/S values and their effects/origins across typed Copy/Move, projected assignment, union/constant storage, aggregate/repeat (before expansion), both conversion directions, raw/volatile copies, byte fill/other writes, delayed checks/SetRef shortcuts, by-value args/returns and pointer-based cross-call effects. Reject uniformly at zero/nonzero extent, including F with N=0 and ZST T; do not rely on a byte loop producing a diagnostic. Retain supported direct W zero-sized behavior, with ABI/protocol controls.
Lost support under A: flat scratch arrays/slices, flat initialized positives, flat-slot bytewise copy/fill and by-value transfers, array-to-flat conversion and flat-derived views/aliases. Conservative origin fallback may reject ordinary pointers, generic helpers, statics, indirect calls or type-erased accesses that cannot be distinguished safely. These compatibility costs require owner review; a direct W array payload and legal ordinary array equality must remain positive controls.
Proposed A controls: each forbidden route, including zero-sized/fully initialized flat storage, must reach exact UnsupportedConstruct before its effect; legal W typed transfer/equality must succeed; W with false data must fail the uninitialized-byte property and padded W must preserve typed-vs-bytewise distinction. Test empty entry, final Assign, terminator-only, unresolved reads/loads and guard-free direct W negatives separately so Unsupported cannot mask a missing check. All NOT_RUN.

**B — retain flat support, if owner chooses it.** Release stays blocked on the raw_eq provenance/init-state model. Keep the expanded domain under review; existing R2 checks alone do not establish correctness or legal equality recovery. Do not substitute A's scope reduction without owner choice.
Required model/evidence: bind each raw_eq operand to possible runtime allocation identities (including anonymous promotions), offset and complete byte extent; preserve actual false state through typed/raw writes, overwrites, reset, aliases, offsets, local/global allocation lifecycle and cross-call argument/return effects. Immutable referent masks need actual allocation bytes including None; normal-return destination setters need proved initialization and ordering. Unknown provenance cannot become true. No model implementation/design is attempted here.
Required B evidence: exact SHA/toolchain/layout, retained intrinsic and transfer MIR, operand→object/offset/extent→witness→last setter/snapshot, matched base/R1/R2/candidate positive and left/right-hole negatives, local/global/promotion, alias/offset/overwrite, cross-call/normal-return and harness-order controls. Type/layout compatibility, a source call graph or raw_eq coexisting in the same body cannot bind runtime state, and producers/consumers may be in different bodies. All proposed runtime/MIR checks are NOT_RUN.

Fallback for both options: unknown shape/layout, compound storage, by-value DST, unresolved alias/call, foreign/heap write/realloc or intercepted/shim protocol without established coverage → retained pre-effect Unsupported; if no guaranteed emitted diagnostic is available, release remains blocked. No silent skip, default initialized mask or absence-of-raw_eq exemption. Zero-byte extent is not an exemption from recognition/protocol; B may admit a supported proven-empty byte access, but unresolved extent stays unsupported/blocked.
Owner must choose A or B and accept its compatibility/proof obligations before W1-R1. R0 stops at this design; completeness, runtime causality and proof acceptance remain unestablished.
