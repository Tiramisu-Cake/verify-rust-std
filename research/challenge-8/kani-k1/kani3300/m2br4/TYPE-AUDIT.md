## B — one integer-expression delta

Old `self.tracked_offset = to_offset + (self.tracked_offset - from_offset)` has usize-usize->usize then usize+usize->usize assigned to usize. New `self.tracked_offset += to_offset - from_offset` has usize-usize->usize then compound usize addition into the same field. No cast/deref/signature change, no use of u64/u128 or overloaded custom arithmetic. Source guard establishes tracked_offset>=from_offset, not to_offset>=from_offset; therefore new subtraction may underflow. Example k=10, from=10, to=0, count=1, N=1 satisfies the source range but legacy 0-10 underflows whereas safe result is0. IDs may designate distinct objects, so offsets need not be ordered. Expected extra overflow properties and earlier failure are diagnostic loss; no algebraic equivalence claim. Retained widths/operators and actual failure IDs UNKNOWN until coordinator projection.
