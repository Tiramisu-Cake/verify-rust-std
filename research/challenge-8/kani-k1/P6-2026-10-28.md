### Findings

1. **The `kani_autoharness` job runs every manual harness of the repository on one runner, in 3 threads, with a
   per-harness timeout of about 10 minutes**, besides the autoharness targets (245 functions, all verified). The
   macOS timeouts: `merge::merge_contract_u64_n16`, `network::insertion_permutation_u64_n12`,
   `network::insert_tail_contract_u64_start1`. In the final CI several SmallSort harnesses took 500–1266 s on macOS.
   Upstream's own comment in `kani.yml` notes the same 10-minute limit on macOS for Unicode functions. Consequence:
   every ordinary SmallSort harness must fit that timeout on the macOS runner under 3-thread load, with margin.
2. **ubuntu autoharness**: after 1829 harnesses (108 SmallSort) the runner was shut down while three SmallSort
   harnesses ran (`merge::merge_contract_u64_n13`, `correct::sorted_permutation_general_stable_u8_n14`,
   `network::mutating_insertion_safety_cell_n11_to12`). Same signature as the first attempt of ubuntu p2 in the
   final CI. Cause not established; memory exhaustion is a hypothesis (final CI ubuntu p2: `kani-driver` 9.8 GiB,
   swap 2.5 GB). Upstream's autoharness jobs on their own PRs take 60–70 min.
3. **`rustc.yml` only checks formatting** (tests are commented out in `check_rustc.sh`). `./x fmt --check`
   skipped untracked files: “fmt: skipped untracked file library/core/src/slice/sort/shared/smallsort/” — the new
   `verify` module is not format-checked by this job; the modified `smallsort.rs` is. Our own rustfmt check (same
   rustfmt `c1070d69`, rust's `rustfmt.toml`) passes on all package files.
